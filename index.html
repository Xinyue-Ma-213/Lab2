Welcome to My Interest Showcase

This page introduces three things that make my free time more enjoyable: exploring the world of Wuthering Waves, listening to Hatsune Miku, and following the adventures of the Straw Hat Pirates in One Piece.

Wuthering Waves

Wuthering Waves is an open-world action game that I enjoy because of its fast-paced combat, detailed environments, and memorable characters. Exploring new areas and building different teams keeps every session interesting.

Hatsune Miku

Hatsune Miku introduced me to the creative world of Vocaloid music. I like how different producers can use the same virtual singer to create songs with completely different moods, stories, and musical styles.
